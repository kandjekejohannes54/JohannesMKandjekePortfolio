About the Project

About SiteSpy

SiteSpy is a mobile-first construction project records and cost estimation app built with Expo React Native, Firebase, and Firestore. It helps construction teams capture measurements, estimate material costs, organise site records, and review project history on any device — even in low-connectivity field conditions.

My Contribution

My Contribution

I reviewed SiteSpy's responsive behaviour and typographic consistency across screen sizes. My work covers spacing.js and typography.js in the theme system, which define the design tokens that keep the app visually consistent. I also authored User_Manual.md for end-user guidance and Testing_Checklist.md that documents mobile layout verification steps. Consistent spacing and clear type hierarchy make the app easier to read in field conditions.

Reflection

My Reflection

Design systems work is often invisible but it multiplies the quality of every screen. I learned how to define spacing scales that work across small and large phones without breaking layouts, and how to choose type sizes that remain legible in bright outdoor light. Writing the user manual forced me to look at SiteSpy from a construction worker's perspective rather than a developer's.
